Our History

A School Built on
Community

John Mitchell Primary School has served the Jeppestown community of Johannesburg for decades, providing quality public primary education to learners from Grade R through to Grade 7. Named in honour of its founding legacy, the school has grown into a cornerstone of the community, shaping generations of young South Africans.

Located at the corner of Marshall and Highgate Streets in Jeppestown, our school draws learners from the surrounding neighbourhoods, offering an inclusive, nurturing environment where every child is seen, known, and supported.

Our staff are not merely educators — they are mentors, coaches, and role models who are deeply invested in the success of every learner in their care.

Our Mission

Developing the Whole Child

To provide a safe, inclusive learning environment that develops each learner's life skills and individual talents — academic, artistic, sporting, or practical — preparing them for the next stage of their educational journey.

Our Vision

A Community of Lifelong Learners

To be a school where every learner, educator, and parent is an active partner in building a culture of excellence, respect, and continuous growth — rooted in the values that have always defined John Mitchell Primary School.

For Parents

Support at Home

Parental Guidance

Your involvement in your child's education makes all the difference. Here are the key ways you can support learning at home and stay connected with the school.

01

Read Daily

Spend at least 20 minutes reading with your child every day. Reading aloud, listening to them read, and discussing stories builds vocabulary, comprehension, and a lifelong love of learning.

02

Homework Routine

Set a consistent homework time each afternoon in a quiet, well-lit space. Check that homework is completed and signed where required. Contact the class teacher if your child is regularly struggling.

03

Communicate Openly

If you have concerns about your child's progress or wellbeing, contact the relevant educator promptly. Educators are available before school (07:20–07:30), during breaks, and after school (14:00–14:30).

04

Attendance Matters

Regular attendance is critical to your child's progress. If your child must be absent, please notify the school office by 08:00. A letter of explanation is required on return.

05

Stay Informed

Check your child's school bag daily for notices, newsletters, and communications from the school. Important information is sent home regularly and is also available from the school office.

06

Model Good Values

Children mirror what they see at home. Reinforce the school's values of respect, responsibility, and kindness in your daily family life. The partnership between home and school is the foundation of every learner's success.

Foundation Phase

The Grade R Programme

Grade R is the vital foundation year of formal schooling. Our programme follows a structured developmental approach across four key stages, ensuring every child is school-ready.

Stage One

Getting to Know You

Settling in, establishing routines, and building trust between learners, educators, and the school environment. Social skills, listening, and following simple instructions are the focus.

Stage Two

Building Foundations

Introduction to phonics, number concepts, fine motor development, and early literacy through play-based and structured learning activities.

Stage Three

Growing Confidence

Learners begin to read simple words, form letters, count and sort objects, and engage in collaborative activities that build confidence and independence.

Stage Four

Ready for Grade 1

Consolidation of all foundational skills. Learners demonstrate school readiness through reading, writing, numeracy, and the ability to function independently in a structured classroom environment.

Admissions

Enrolment Process

How to Apply

Enrolling at John Mitchell Primary School is straightforward. Follow the steps below to secure your child's place.

01
Contact the School
Call or email the school office to confirm availability for your child's grade. Speak with Mrs. Kleynhans in Admissions.
02
Collect Application Forms
Visit the school office to collect the official application forms. Office hours are Monday to Friday, 07:00–14:30.
03
Submit Documents
Complete the forms and submit them together with all required supporting documents listed below.
04
Confirmation
Once your application is reviewed, the school will contact you to confirm your child's enrolment and provide a start date.
Contact admissions directly

What to Bring

Required Documents

Please ensure you have all of the following documents ready when submitting your application.

Certified copy of the learner's birth certificate
Certified copy of parent / guardian's ID document
Proof of residential address (utility bill or lease agreement)
Most recent school report (Grade 1 and above)
Immunisation / vaccination card
Transfer card from previous school (if applicable)
Any relevant medical or special needs documentation
Completed application form (available from the school office)
